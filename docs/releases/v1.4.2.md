## LifeSync 1.4.2

Fixes payment detection for Paytm alerts and SBI UPI messages.

### 💰 Money Hub

**When Android hides a payment alert**
- On Android 15 and newer, Android can hide the contents of "sensitive" notifications - including some Paytm payment alerts - from other apps. You still see the full alert, but LifeSync only got "Sensitive notification content hidden".
- LifeSync now spots this and immediately looks for the **matching bank SMS** instead, so the payment is still offered to you (as long as your bank texts you for it).
- Settings → Finance → Auto-detect Payments now shows **"Android is hiding payment alerts"** when this happens, with how to let LifeSync read them directly.

**SBI UPI messages**
- Fixed: SBI UPI messages like *"A/C X8019 credited by 50.00"* had the account digits read as the amount (₹8,019 instead of ₹50).
- Fixed: SBI UPI messages (and other banks' newer sender IDs) were skipped by "Scan SMS now". More bank sender IDs are recognised now.

### Install

Download **`LifeSync-v1.4.2.apk`** below and open it on your Android phone. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your data.
