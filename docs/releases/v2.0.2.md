## LifeSync 2.0.2

Reminders now play the tone you picked, and you can tick off a habit straight from its reminder.

### ✅ Habits

- **Done and Snooze on reminders.** Tap **Done ✓** to mark the habit complete for today without opening LifeSync, or **Snooze 10 min** to be reminded again shortly. For habits you do several times a day, each reminder's Done counts once.
- **Test Notification** in Settings now sends a sample reminder with your tone and the new buttons, so you can check how reminders will look and sound.

### 🔔 Reminder tones

- **Your chosen tones now play.** Habit and water reminders were playing the phone's default sound instead of the tone you picked. Your reminders are set up again automatically the first time you open this version.
- **Tone previews are easier to hear.** Tapping a tone plays it at your notification volume, so you hear it even when media volume is off.
- **Snoozed water reminders** use your water reminder tone too.

### Install

Download **`LifeSync-v2.0.2.apk`** below and open it on your Android phone. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your data.
