## LifeSync 2.2.0

Habit reminders now know when you're done, the home screen widgets scroll properly, and every theme comes in light and dark.

### ✅ Habits

- **No more reminders for habits you've already done.** Once you complete a habit for the day, its remaining reminders for that day are skipped. For habits you do several times a day, reminders stop once you reach the day's target. Undo a completion and the reminders come back.
- Reminders are set up a few days ahead and refreshed each time you open LifeSync, so open the app at least once a week to keep them coming.

### 🎨 Look and feel

- **Light and dark for every theme.** Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Rose and the rest now each have a light and a dark version. The theme picker shows them in two rows, Dark and Light, so the matching pair sits side by side.

### 📣 Announcements

- LifeSync can now show you occasional announcements inside the app, such as news about new features. You'll see each one once.

### 🐛 Fixes

- **Widgets:** the Today's habits and Money widgets no longer cut off the bottom of their lists. You can now scroll all the way to the last habit or transaction, at any widget size.
- **Widgets:** the habit progress bar no longer spills past the edge of the widget after you resize it.

### Install

Download **`LifeSync-v2.2.0.apk`** below and open it on your Android phone. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your data.
