## LifeSync 2.2.1

Smoother, more responsive motion across the app.

### 🎨 Look and feel

- **Swipe the side menu closed.** The menu now follows your finger, and a quick flick is enough to close it. It opens and closes with a smoother, natural motion, and the background dims gradually with it.
- **Habit sheet feels lighter.** The add/edit habit sheet slides up while the background fades in place, and a quick downward flick dismisses it. Tapping outside or pressing Back now closes it smoothly instead of cutting off.
- **Snappier pop-ups.** Confirmation and alert dialogs appear and disappear faster, without the wobble.
- **Sort menu** now grows out of the button that opens it.
- **Habit check-offs** respond the moment your finger touches them.
- The Workouts tab underline glides between tabs, even when you tap quickly.
- The buttons at the top of the Habits screen are easier to hit.

All new motion respects your phone's "Remove animations" setting.

### Install

Download **`LifeSync-v2.2.1.apk`** below and open it on your Android phone. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your data.
