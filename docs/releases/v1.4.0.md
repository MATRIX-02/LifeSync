## LifeSync 1.4.0

Money Hub can now spot your payments for you.

### 💰 Money Hub

**Auto-detect payments (Android)**
- When you pay or get paid, LifeSync reads the alert from your bank SMS, bank app or UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and more) and sends you a notification like **"₹250 spent at Swiggy · Tap to add"** - even when the app is closed.
- Tap it to open a ready-filled form: amount, merchant, the time you paid, a suggested category and the right account. Nothing is saved until you press **Add**.
- Payments waiting for you show as **"N detected payments to review"** at the top of Money Hub.
- If you missed an alert (phone off, notification swiped away), LifeSync checks your recent bank SMS when you open the app.
- One payment that shows up twice - once from your UPI app and once as a bank SMS - is only offered once.
- OTPs, payment requests, failed payments and "will be debited" reminders are ignored, and so are messages from people.
- LifeSync learns as you go: the category you pick for a merchant is suggested next time.
- Everything is read on your phone. No message text leaves the device unless you add the payment.
- Turn it on in **Settings → Finance → Auto-detect Payments** (you'll be asked to allow Notification access).

**Card / account numbers on accounts**
- Accounts have a new **"Card / account ending in"** field. Add the last 4 digits from your bank SMS (e.g. **1234**, or several like **1234, 5678**) and detected payments go straight to that account.
- Or tick **"Always use this account for ••1234"** the first time you add a payment - it's saved to the account for next time.

### 🏋️ Workouts
- Timer and rest-timer notifications now use their own notification channel, so you can adjust their sound and vibration in Android settings.

### Install

Download **`LifeSync-v1.4.0.apk`** below and open it on your Android phone. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your data.
