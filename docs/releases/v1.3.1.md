## LifeSync 1.3.1

A small update so every module goes by its real name.

### ✨ Names
- Module pages now show their proper names: **Daily Rituals**, **FitZone** and **Money Hub** (instead of "Habits Tracker", "Workout Tracker" and "Finance Tracker").
- The Cloud Sync list in Settings uses the same names.

### Install

Download **`LifeSync-v1.3.1.apk`** below and open it on your Android phone. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your data.
