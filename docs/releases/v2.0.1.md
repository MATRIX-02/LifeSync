## LifeSync 2.0.1

See your update download, and check for updates any time.

### ✨ General

- **Download progress.** When you install an update, a card shows how far the download has got, in % and MB. You can **Hide** it and keep using LifeSync, or **Cancel**. Progress also shows in your notifications.
- **If a download fails**, tap **Retry**. If you closed Android's install prompt by mistake, tap **Open installer again**.
- **Check for updates** in Settings → About. It shows what's new in the latest version, with a **Download & install** button.

### Install

Download **`LifeSync-v2.0.1.apk`** below and open it on your Android phone. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your data. If you're on 2.0.0, you'll also get this automatically the next time you restart LifeSync.
