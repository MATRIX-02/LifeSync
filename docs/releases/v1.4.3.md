## LifeSync 1.4.3

Fewer false payment alerts, quicker ways to handle detected payments, and automatic updates from now on.

### 💰 Money Hub

**Fewer false payment alerts**
- WhatsApp messages are no longer read as payments. Business broadcasts like *"Rs. 300 just landed in your wallet!"* were being offered as money received. Payments made with WhatsApp Pay are still picked up from your bank's SMS.
- Promotional SMS (senders ending in **-P**, like `JD-JIOMRT-P`) are ignored. Banks never send payment alerts from these.
- Offers from payment apps - "claim now", vouchers, coupons, loan offers, links - are no longer treated as payments, even when they say "received ₹…".

**Buttons on detected payments**
- The "Tap to add" notification now has **Add** and **Ignore** buttons. **Ignore** clears it without opening LifeSync, and it won't come back.

**Other improvements**
- The payment review sheet scrolls properly, stays above the keyboard, and keeps its buttons on screen on small phones.
- Account cards in My Accounts show the account's last 4 digits (e.g. **bank ••1234**).

### ✨ General

- **Automatic updates.** Most future updates install on their own the next time you open LifeSync. When a bigger update needs a new download, you'll get a notification - tap it, then tap **Install**. The first time, Android asks you to allow LifeSync to install apps.
- **Landscape.** LifeSync now rotates with your phone.
- Settings → Preferences shows the correct app version.

### Install

Download **`LifeSync-v1.4.3.apk`** below and open it on your Android phone. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your data. Sign in after installing so you receive update notifications.
