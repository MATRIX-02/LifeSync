## LifeSync 2.3.0

A big Money Hub update: faster transactions, sorting, month-by-month budgets and bills, and savings that are properly tracked.

### 💰 Money Hub

- **Transactions load as you scroll.** The list loads your most recent transactions first and fetches more as you scroll down, so it stays quick however much history you have.
- **Sort transactions.** Open the filter and choose Newest, Oldest, Amount: high to low, or Amount: low to high.
- **This month and last month filters.** Pick the current or previous month (for example "November" or "October") in the period filter.
- **Closing balances you can trust.** Each transaction's closing balance is now saved and kept correct automatically, even when you add, edit or delete an older entry.
- **Budgets by month.** Use the new month picker to look back at any month. Each month remembers the budget limits it had at the time, so changing a budget today doesn't rewrite your history.
- **Bills by month.** See the bills due in any month, paid and unpaid. Unpaid bills from earlier months stay visible in the current month so nothing slips through.
- **Savings goals now show in Transactions.** Adding money to a goal or withdrawing it now appears in your transaction list and updates your account correctly, including credit cards. Savings don't count as spending in your budgets or analytics.
- **Safer goal deletion.** If a goal still holds money, you're asked to withdraw it first so it isn't lost.

### 🐛 Fixes

- Searching transactions for a number now matches the exact amount.
- Money moved into or out of a savings goal could change your account balance without leaving any record. Past moves have been added to your transaction history.
- Contributing or withdrawing an empty, zero or negative amount is no longer accepted, and you can't withdraw more than a goal holds.

### Install

Download **`LifeSync-v2.3.0.apk`** below and open it on your Android phone. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your data.
