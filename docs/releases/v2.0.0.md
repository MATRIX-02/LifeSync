## LifeSync 2.0.0

A rebuilt Split Expenses, a new water tracker with smarter reminders, your own AI allowance, and custom reminder tones. This version also removes Study Hub.

### 💰 Money Hub: Split Expenses

**See where you stand**
- The Split Expenses home shows how much you're owed and how much you owe across everything, with **Groups**, **Friends** and **Activity** tabs.
- **Friends:** split a one-off cost with someone without making a group. Each friend shows one balance across every group you share, and **Settle up** clears it in one go.
- **Activity:** see who added, edited or deleted what, and who paid whom.
- **Search** every expense by name, person, amount or place.
- **Charts:** spending by category and by month, and who paid versus each person's share.

**Adding expenses**
- **Edit any expense.** No more deleting and re-adding to fix a typo.
- **More than one person can pay** for a single expense.
- **Leave people out of an equal split.** Untick anyone who wasn't there.
- **Split a bill by item.** Take a photo of the bill and LifeSync reads the items, or add them yourself. Tap who had what, and tax is shared fairly.
- **Repeating expenses:** rent or subscriptions can repeat every week, month or year.
- **Comments** on any expense.
- Each group can have a **default split** (for example, always 60/40).

**Settling up**
- **Pay via UPI:** opens GPay, PhonePe or Paytm with the amount filled in. Add UPI IDs in a group's Members tab.
- **Remind** someone who owes you, by notification if they use LifeSync, or a message you can send on WhatsApp.
- Payments can now be **edited or deleted**.
- Turn **Simplify debts** off to see who owes whom for each expense.

**Groups**
- **Archive** old groups to tidy your list without losing anything.
- **Export** a group's expenses as a spreadsheet.
- People you invite can be added to expenses straight away, before they accept.
- Two people adding expenses at the same moment no longer overwrite each other.

### 🏋️ FitZone: NutriPlan

**A new water tracker**
- **Water** is now the first tab: a glass that fills as you drink, one-tap **Glass** and **Bottle** buttons, **Undo**, and your last 7 days at a glance.

**Water reminders**
- Choose your hours, how often, which days, and the tone.
- Reminders now arrive at the right times, and say how much you should have had by then.
- **Log water from the notification:** tap **+250 ml** or **+500 ml** without opening the app, or **Snooze** for 15 minutes.
- A new **Water Pour** tone, used for water reminders by default.

### ✅ Habits

- **Choose a tone** for each habit's reminder or alarm, or set defaults for all habits in Settings.

### ✨ AI insights

- **Use your own free Gemini key** for your own AI allowance instead of sharing one with everyone: Settings → AI Usage → **Your Gemini Key**. It takes about 2 minutes and no card. It's also needed for scanning bills.
- Without a key, every account gets a daily allowance of AI analyses. **Check Usage** shows how many you have left.

### ⚠️ Changes to be aware of

- **Study Hub has been removed.** Your study data is still stored in your account, but it can no longer be viewed in the app.
- **Buying a paid plan in the app is paused** while payments move to a more secure system.
