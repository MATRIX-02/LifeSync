## LifeSync 2.2.2

A quick fix for the side menu.

### 🐛 Fixes

- **Side menu taps work first time.** Right after opening the side menu, tapping Daily Rituals, FitZone or Money Hub could do nothing, so you had to tap again. Each tap now works the first time.
- The add/edit habit sheet had the same problem for a moment after opening. That's fixed too.

### Install

Download **`LifeSync-v2.2.2.apk`** below and open it on your Android phone. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your data.
