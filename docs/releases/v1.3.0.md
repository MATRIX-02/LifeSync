## LifeSync 1.3.0

A big update to **Money Hub** and **Workouts**, plus a calendar fix for habits.

### 💰 Money Hub

**Your own categories**
- Create custom expense and income categories, and hide built-in ones you don't use (Settings → Finance → Categories).
- Custom categories now work everywhere: transactions, budgets, bills, savings goals and split expenses.
- New income categories: Bonus, Cashback & Rewards, Allowance, Reimbursement, Sale of Items and more.
- New transactions no longer default to a category you've hidden.

**Budgets, bills and savings goals**
- Budgets can now be **edited** (category and monthly limit).
- Savings goals can now be **edited** (name, target, deadline, priority, category).
- Savings goals show a **monthly plan**: how much to save each month and week to hit your deadline, whether you're ahead, on track or behind, and when you'll finish at your current pace.
- Bills can repeat **weekly, monthly or yearly**, not just monthly, and can have a category.

**Analytics, rebuilt**
- **Money Health Score** (out of 100) with a plain-language breakdown: money left over, backup savings, staying in budget, debts, credit card use and late bills.
- **This Month**: how much you can still spend, per day, after bills and savings goals - plus where you're likely to end the month.
- **What You Should Do**: personalised suggestions with real amounts (e.g. which category to trim, how much to pay off your card).
- Budget forecasts, spending changes vs. a normal month, and a summary of what you owe and own.
- Date filter is now **Last 24 hours / 7 days / 30 days / 3 months / 12 months / All time**, plus a **custom date range**.
- Removed the daily-average stat.

**Transactions**
- Set or change a transaction's **date and time**.
- Fixed: transactions added late at night could be saved under the wrong day.
- Transaction list date filters (today, 7 / 30 / 365 days, custom).

### 🏋️ Workouts

**Exercises measured the right way**
- Each exercise is now tracked by what it actually measures: **weight × reps**, **reps**, **time** (plank, stretches, yoga holds) or **distance + time** (running, cycling, rowing).
- **Set timer**: tap ▶ on a timed set. It counts down from your target, buzzes for the last 3 seconds and beeps at zero, then keeps counting overtime (+0:12) until you stop it - so a forgotten timer still records your real time.
- **Rest timer** now buzzes and beeps when it's done.
- **Notifications** for both timers when your phone is locked or you're in another app.
- History, stats and the finish screen show time and distance (e.g. "3 × 45s", "5 km · 28:30") instead of "0 kg".
- **Personal records** now actually save - heaviest weight, most reps, longest hold or longest distance.

**Form videos**
- Every built-in exercise now has a **YouTube form video** from trusted coaches and physiotherapists, shown in the exercise info sheet.
- The **ⓘ info button** is now also on exercises in the plan editor and during a workout.

**New exercises and templates**
- 🏃 **Running**: Runner's Strength, Shin Splint Prevention, Achilles & Calf Care, Pre-Run Warm-Up, Post-Run Mobility - with tibialis raises, soleus raises, eccentric heel drops, Copenhagen planks, single-leg RDLs and more.
- 🦶 **Feet & Arches**: Flat Foot / Arch Strength and Plantar Fasciitis Relief - short foot, toe yoga, towel scrunches, banded inversion and more.
- New **Shins** and **Feet & Ankles** muscle groups.

### ✅ Habits
- Fixed: days a habit isn't scheduled on (e.g. weekends for a weekday habit) showed as completed in the calendar and list views. They now show as rest days, and you can still log them as a bonus.
- Fixed: a multi-time-per-day habit could lose its chosen weekdays when restored from older data.

### ⚙️ Settings
- Reorganised Settings, with new Finance (categories, AI reading of bank SMS) and AI Usage sections.

### For self-hosters: database migrations

Run these in the Supabase SQL editor before using this version (in order):

1. `supabase/migrations/20261007_finance_categories.sql` - custom/hidden categories
2. `supabase/migrations/20261007_finance_category_columns.sql` - allows any category key on transactions, budgets and bills, and adds a category to savings goals

The app keeps working without them, but custom categories won't save until they're applied.
