## LifeSync 1.4.1

Makes payment detection easier to set up and check, and recognises more UPI apps.

### 💰 Money Hub

**Check that detection works**
- New **Test detection** button (Settings → Finance → Auto-detect Payments). It tells you within seconds whether LifeSync can see notifications on your phone - and if not, exactly what to change.
- New **Recent alerts seen** list: the latest payment alerts LifeSync received and what it did with each (offered to you, already seen, not a payment). If a payment didn't show up, this tells you why.

**More apps, better reading**
- Now also recognises **BHIM, Paytm for Business, Airtel Thanks, Navi, super.money, Jupiter, Fi and slice**, along with Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay, Amazon Pay, CRED, MobiKwik, Freecharge and WhatsApp Pay.
- Payment alerts from other UPI apps are picked up too, when they clearly show a UPI payment with a reference number.
- "Rahul paid you ₹50" is now correctly recorded as money **received**.
- Cleaner names for who you paid or who paid you.

### Not getting payments detected?

Open **Settings → Finance → Auto-detect Payments** and tap **Test detection**. If it says LifeSync didn't receive the test:
- Turn **Notification access** off and on again for LifeSync.
- If the switch is greyed out or says *Restricted setting*: long-press the LifeSync icon → **App info** → **⋮** → **Allow restricted settings**.
- **App info → Battery → No restrictions**. On Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo and Realme, also turn on **Autostart**.

Download **`LifeSync-v1.4.1.apk`** below and open it on your Android phone. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your data.
