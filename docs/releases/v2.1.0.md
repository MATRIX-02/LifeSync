## LifeSync 2.1.0

A fresh new look: a new logo, themes you can choose, and home screen widgets for your habits and money.

### 🎨 Look and feel

- **New logo.** LifeSync has a new icon on your home screen, splash screen and notifications.
- **Themes.** Settings › Appearance now has a theme picker instead of the dark mode switch. **Lotus**, inspired by the new logo, is the new default, and the original purple look is still there as **Classic**. You can also pick **AMOLED** (true black), **Ocean**, **Forest**, **Sunset** and **Rose**, each with a light version where it fits, or **Auto** to follow your phone's light/dark setting.
- **Accent colour.** Choose the colour for buttons, highlights and progress bars, on top of any theme.
- **Text size.** Make all text in the app smaller or larger: Small, Default, Large or Extra large.
- **App icon.** Pick how LifeSync looks on your home screen: Midnight, Light, Black or Ocean. Your launcher may take a few seconds to show the new icon.

### 📱 Home screen widgets

- **Today's habits.** See today's habits and how many you've done, and tick them off straight from your home screen without opening LifeSync. The list scrolls when you have many habits.
- **Money.** See what you've spent and earned this month, today's spending, and your recent transactions.
- To add one, long-press your home screen, tap **Widgets**, and find **LifeSync**. Both widgets match the theme you pick in the app.

### 🔄 App updates

- **Check for updates** now looks like your phone's own system update screen. It shows your current version, when it last checked, the size of a new update, and what's new — even when you're already up to date.

### 🐛 Fixes

- On some phones, including Samsung Galaxy models, the last letter of bold words was cut off (for example "Profil" instead of "Profile"). Text now shows in full.

### Install

Download **`LifeSync-v2.1.0.apk`** below and open it on your Android phone. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your data.
